# Product images

- The first image of each product should be standardized across the store. Use one style and one aspect ratio across the catalog, so collection pages read as a grid and not a collage. Square is the safe default.
- Each product needs at least three images: the main shot, a close-up of the material or detail, and a shot that shows scale or use.
- Remove all duplicate images.
- Link each variant that looks different to its own images, so choosing the variant switches the photo.
- Use at least 2000 px on the long edge so zoom works.
- No image should be smaller than 100px.
- Write alt text for someone who can't see the image: product type, color, and what the shot shows. No keyword lists and no file names.
- Optimize images for size and quality.
- Use latest gen image formats - WEBP or JPEG XL or AVIF.
- Use descriptive filenames with a unique identifier.
- Don't use supplier images that carry watermarks or another store's branding.
