# Product titles

- Names should be as specific and explicit. "Aurora" fails. "Aurora linen duvet cover" passes.
- Typical order: brand or model name, then product type, then the attributes that separates the product from its siblings.
- Put the words shoppers search first. Search results and shopping feeds cut titles at about 70 characters.
- Leave variant values such as color and size out of the title. The variant picker shows them.
- Use one title pattern per store (or at least per category).
- No prices, promo words ("Sale", "New", "Best"). They go stale and they describe the offer, not the product.
- Every title is unique. Two products with the same title are duplicates or variants of one product.
