# Product variants

- A variant is a choice the shopper makes after deciding to buy: size, color, pack size. If shoppers compare two items as different things, they are separate products.
- Make color a variant by default. Split a color into its own product only when shoppers search for it by name.
- Products with identical titles and different options are one product with variants. Merge them.
- Use two option axes at most, three in rare cases. A product that needs more is usually several products.
- Give each axis one name across the whole store: "Size", never "size", "Sizes", or "Größe" on another product.
- Give each value one spelling and one scale across the store. Pick "S, M, L" or "Small, Medium, Large", not both.
- Sort values in their natural order (small to large), never alphabetically.
- Every variant that looks different needs its own image.
- Delete or hide variants that are permanently out of stock.
- Don't use variants for add-ons such as gift wrap or warranties.
